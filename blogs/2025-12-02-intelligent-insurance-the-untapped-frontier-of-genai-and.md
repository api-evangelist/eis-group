For more than a decade, the worlds of finance and technology have been coming together in ever more powerful ways. Digital banking, open finance, and embedded payments have transformed how consumers manage their money and how financial institutions create value. Yet insurance, perhaps the last financial frontier, has barely begun to realize this potential.
